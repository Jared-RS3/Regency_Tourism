STARTING POINT

What Regency Asked For

The brief is not a request for a brochure. It asks for a business development instrument — a website whose job is to generate international B2B enquiries from travel agencies, tour operators, DMCs and corporate planners, and to make Regency legible as a professional Cape Town ground handler rather than a local tour company.

The brief, condensed

  1. 01Position Regency as a Cape Town land-arrangements and destination management partner, not a retail tour seller.
  2. 02Speak to an international trade audience — GCC, UK, USA, Canada, Europe, India, Australia, Asia and Africa.
  3. 03Carry a nine-item menu: Home, About, Services, Destinations, Itineraries, Trade Partners, Groups & MICE, VIP & Corporate, Contact.
  4. 04Drive every page toward two actions — Request a Quote and Become a Trade Partner — and make both trackable.
  5. 05Build for search: individual pages for the money terms, not one page carrying everything.
  6. 06Prepare for translated B2B landing pages, CRM, conversion pixels and a partner-facing layer over time.

Non-negotiables

  • Mobile-first and fast — most trade browsing happens between meetings, on a phone.
  • Premium and spacious. International, not regional.
  • Deep blue, black, white, subtle grey. Authentic Cape Town photography over stock.
  • Minimal animation. Strong typography.
  • HTTPS, sitemap, schema, SEO-friendly URLs.
  • WhatsApp reachable from every page.
  • Every call to action measurable.
→

This proposal quotes Phase 01 only — the website and the lead capture that has to work on day one, at R25,000. The rest of the brief is real work with a real cost, so it is priced item by item on the roadmap page rather than left as an open question. Nothing on that page is committed by signing this one.

THE STRATEGIC PROBLEM

You Sell The
Destination. We
Handle The Ground.

The website has two audiences with opposite needs, and most tourism sites fail by choosing one. A tour operator in Dubai is assessing whether Regency can be trusted with forty delegates. A traveller from London wants to know what a day in the Winelands looks like. The same nine pages must serve both without diluting either.

How one site carries both

  • The trade audience owns the structure. Navigation, calls to action and the permanent header buttons are written for a professional buyer.
  • The traveller content is the proof. Destinations and itineraries exist to demonstrate ground capability, not to sell seats. A tour operator reads them as evidence.
  • Trade Partners, Groups & MICE and VIP & Corporate are the conversion pages. They carry the forms, the credentials and the response commitments.
  • One message binds it. You sell the destination. We handle the ground.

Five seconds on the homepage

A trade visitor must absorb all of this above the fold and in the strip immediately beneath it:

  • We are based in Cape Town.
  • We handle the complete ground operation.
  • We work with international travel companies.
  • FIT, groups, corporate, MICE and VIP.
  • Programmes are built to order.
  • There is one person to contact, and they answer.
→

The single most valuable thing this website can do is make an unfamiliar overseas agency comfortable enough to send a first enquiry. Everything in Phase 01 is pointed at that moment — which is why the enquiry forms and the lead record are quoted as build work, not treated as a plugin.

OUR RECOMMENDATION

Concept 04,
With One Borrowing

Why this one

  • It answers "where" before it answers anything else. An agency in Riyadh or Toronto recognises the destination in under a second — which is the whole positioning.
  • The two calls to action sit as equals. The brief asks for a permanent quote button and a permanent partner button. This is the only concept that already gives both the same weight.
  • The wordmark works as architecture, not decoration. It establishes scale without a second typeface.
  • It holds up in a boardroom. Concept 05 is the warmest, but a scattered collage reads consumer. Regency is being assessed as a supplier.

The borrowing

Concept 05's trust strip — 1,000+ Cape Town experiences · trusted by 200+ trade partners · 100% tailor-made itineraries — is the strongest single element across all five. It moves directly beneath the hero on Concept 04 as the trust bar the brief calls for, carrying real figures.

PHASE 01 SCOPE

Nine Pages And
Two Forms

A fast, mobile-first, statically hosted website — the entire menu the brief specifies, both enquiry paths, and the measurement needed to know whether either is working.

The nine pages

  • Home — hero, trust bar, ground-support strip, why Regency, services, partner CTA.
  • About — the Cape Town team, how the operation works, credentials.
  • Services — nine service blocks from land arrangements through to security and protection.
  • Destinations — Cape Town, Peninsula, Winelands, Garden Route, wildlife, cultural.
  • Itineraries — sample programmes as evidence of ground capability.
  • Trade Partners — the partner application form and what working with Regency involves.
  • Groups & MICE — capacity, logistics, delegation handling.
  • VIP & Corporate — premium transport, executive assistance, security coordination.
  • Contact — WhatsApp, email, quote form, map, social.

On every page

  • Permanent Request a Quote and Become a Partner buttons in the header.
  • A closing partner call-to-action band before the footer.
  • Floating WhatsApp button, click-tracked.
  • Unique title, meta description and SEO-friendly URL.

Technical baseline

  • Mobile-first, tested at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390 px.
  • Compressed, correctly sized and lazy-loaded imagery.
  • HTTPS, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, canonical URLs.
  • Organization and LocalBusiness schema markup.
  • Google Analytics 4 and Search Console connected and verified.
  • Static hosting — no CMS to patch, nothing to be hacked.
→

Content. Phase 01 shapes and lays out copy that Regency supplies, and writes the connective headings and calls to action. Full professional B2B copywriting for all nine pages is priced separately on the roadmap — it is the single most common cause of a launch date slipping.

WHERE THE ENQUIRIES GO

One Enquiry,
Four Steps

The brief asks for basic CRM. In Phase 01 that means something specific and deliberately modest: no enquiry is ever lost, every enquiry is answered immediately, and every enquiry lands in one structured, exportable record that a real CRM can import later without rework.

01

Submit

A trade visitor completes the quote form or the partner application. Fields are validated in the browser; spam is filtered without a visible challenge.

02

Confirm

The enquirer receives a branded auto-reply within seconds, stating a response commitment. Overseas agencies judge suppliers on exactly this.

03

Notify

Regency receives the full enquiry by email — and optionally by WhatsApp — routed to the right inbox by enquiry type.

04

Record

The submission is written to a structured lead sheet, one row per enquiry, timestamped and attributed to the page it came from.

What every lead record holds

  • Contact name
  • Company / agency
  • Country and market
  • Enquiry type
  • Traveller type — FIT, group, MICE, VIP
  • Passenger count
  • Travel dates
  • Services required
  • Message
  • Source page
  • Referrer / campaign
  • Timestamp

Said plainly

This is a lead capture system, not a CRM. It will not assign owners, chase follow-ups, score deals or report on a pipeline. It gives Regency one reliable place where every enquiry lands, with the fields a CRM needs already in the right columns.

When enquiry volume justifies it, a HubSpot or Zoho pipeline is priced on the roadmap at R17,000. Nothing built in Phase 01 is thrown away in that migration.

THE HONEST PAGE

What Phase 01
Does Not Do

A R25,000 website is a strong, credible, fast-loading trade presence. It is not a platform. These are the things it will not do, stated now rather than discovered in month two.

Not in this phase

  • No online booking or payment. Enquiries only. Regency quotes and contracts offline, as trade business is done.
  • No CMS. Content changes come through Daybreak, under a care plan or hourly. A CMS is a deliberate later decision, not an oversight.
  • No CRM pipeline. A structured lead sheet, not deal stages, owners or automated follow-up.
  • No partner login or rate cards. Trade Partners is an application form in Phase 01.
  • No downloadable itinerary PDFs and no itinerary filtering.
  • No translated pages. Professional English only.
  • No live review feed. Reviews are placed manually from what Regency supplies.
  • No pixels beyond GA4. Meta and LinkedIn tags are on the roadmap.

And two things nobody can promise

Search rankings. Phase 01 builds the technical foundation — structure, speed, schema, indexing — that ranking requires. It does not produce rankings. Competing for Cape Town DMC against established operators needs the copywriting and landing-page work on the roadmap, and months of it. Anyone quoting a ranking guarantee is selling something else.

Enquiry volume. The website converts the traffic it receives. It does not create traffic. Trade enquiries in the first months will come from Regency's own outreach, LinkedIn and trade shows, with the website closing them. That is exactly what it is designed for.
